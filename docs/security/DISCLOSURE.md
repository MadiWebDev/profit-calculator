# Vulnerability Disclosure Policy (S5)

- Report security issues via the contact on /.well-known/security.txt
  (https://www.getprofitcalc.com/contact). Do not post exploit details
  publicly before a fix.
- We triage within 5 business days and aim to fix confirmed issues
  within 90 days, sooner for critical issues.
- Do not access other merchants' data, do not degrade the service
  (no DoS testing), and do not use automated scanners against
  production without prior written permission.
- We do not currently run a paid bug bounty. Good-faith researchers
  who follow this policy will be credited (with permission) in the
  release notes for the fix.
- Out of scope: marketing-site content issues, missing security
  headers on third-party embeds, and social-engineering reports
  without a technical vulnerability.
